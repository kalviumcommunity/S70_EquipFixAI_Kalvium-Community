# PLANT SAFETY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP-SP-12)
Title: Hazardous Energy Control & Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) for Machine Tool Spindle & Drive Systems
Department: Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) / Plant Maintenance
Document ID: SOP-EHS-SP12-2026
Compliance: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 / ISO 14118

SECTION 1: MANDATORY SAFETY PREREQUISITES & SCOPE
This safety procedure applies to all maintenance technicians, electricians, and supervisors performing mechanical disassembly, 
spindle replacement, belt adjustments, or electrical troubleshooting on CNC machining centers (including CNC-01, CNC-02, CNC-04).

⚠️ DANGER: HIGH VOLTAGE AND STORED MECHANICAL HAZARD
CNC spindle motors operate at 480V 3-Phase AC power and retain stored electrical charge in DC-bus capacitor banks for up to 10 minutes 
after power cutoff. High-pressure pneumatic counterbalances also maintain stored kinetic energy. 
Failure to follow Lockout/Tagout will result in electrocution, crushing injury, or severe limb amputation.

SECTION 2: REQUIRED PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
All personnel entering the spindle maintenance boundary must wear:
1. ANSI Z87.1 certified impact-resistant safety glasses with side shields.
2. EN 388 Level 4 cut-resistant nitrile-coated mechanical gloves.
3. Steel-toe safety boots with ASTM F2413 electrical hazard rating.
4. Arc-rated face shield if opening primary 480V distribution cabinet MDP-3.

SECTION 3: STEP-BY-STEP LOCKOUT/TAGOUT ISOLATION PROTOCOL
Step 1: Notify the machine operator and shift supervisor before initiating machine shutdown.
Step 2: Press the emergency stop push-button on the main CNC operator console to park the Z-axis carriage.
Step 3: ⚠️ WARNING: Switch main disconnect circuit breaker on Plant Distribution Panel MDP-3 (Breaker #14) to the OFF position.
Step 4: Attach approved red lockout hasp, personal safety padlock (Key Code LOTO-EHS), and high-visibility danger tag with technician name and date.
Step 5: ⚠️ WARNING: Test incoming terminals L1, L2, L3 using a calibrated digital multimeter. Verify zero electrical voltage (< 1.0 VAC/VDC).
Step 6: ⚠️ CAUTION: Close the manual air supply ball valve on the secondary pneumatic manifold and depress the manual dump valve to bleed pressure down to exactly 0 PSI.
Step 7: Verify zero energy state by attempting to power on the machine control console. Machine must remain completely unpowered.
Step 8: Only after steps 1 through 7 are verified may mechanical disassembly or bearing replacement begin.
