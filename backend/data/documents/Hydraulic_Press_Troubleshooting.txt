# HYDRAULIC STAMPING PRESS TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE (PRESS-01)
Equipment: 200-Ton Hydraulic Stamping Press
Document ID: TS-PRESS-2026-V1
Classification: Troubleshooting Guide & Diagnostics

SECTION 1: HYDRAULIC PRESSURE LOSS AND CYCLE TIME ANOMALIES
Symptom: Ram fails to achieve full 200-ton tonnage or ram extension stroke stalls midway.

Root Causes:
1. Main proportional pressure relief valve spool binding due to particulate contamination in hydraulic oil (ISO 4406 cleanliness class > 19/17/14).
2. Piston seal extrusion or degradation on the main cylinder, leading to internal bypass oil leakage.
3. Variable displacement axial piston pump cavitation caused by clogged suction strainer or aerated oil reservoir.

Recommended Checks & Corrective Actions:
1. ⚠️ WARNING: Depressurize high-pressure hydraulic accumulator before loosening gauge ports.
2. Install 0-400 bar digital test pressure gauge on primary manifold test port TP-1.
3. Measure holding pressure during clamping stroke. Minimum acceptable pressure is 210 bar.
4. If pressure drops below 180 bar, inspect proportional relief valve VR-1 and flush valve spool with mineral spirits.
5. Inspect return line filter differential pressure indicator; replace 10-micron filter element (Part: FLT-HYD-10U) if red flag is raised.
