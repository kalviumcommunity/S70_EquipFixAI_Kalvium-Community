# FLUID POWER ZERO-ENERGY CONTROL STANDARD (SOP-LOTO-HYD-04)
Title: Hydraulic Stamping Press & Pneumatic Subsystem Lockout/Tagout De-energization SOP
Document ID: SOP-HYD-LOTO-2026
Standard Compliance: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 / ISO 4413 Hydraulic Fluid Power / ISO 4414 Pneumatics
Equipment Association: PRESS-01 (200-Ton Hydraulic Press) / CNC-04 (Pneumatic Counterbalance)
Classification: Plant Safety Standard Operating Procedure

SECTION 1: HAZARDS OF STORED HYDRAULIC & PNEUMATIC ENERGY
Fluid power systems store energy in compressed liquids, compressed air reservoirs, pressurized bladder accumulators, 
and elevated mechanical rams under gravity load.
⚠️ DANGER: HIGH PRESSURE FLUID INJECTION & PINCH HAZARDS
- Operating hydraulic system pressure reaches 210 Bar (3,050 PSI). High pressure pinhole leaks can penetrate skin and tissue, causing fatal bloodstream toxicity or severe amputation.
- Compressed pneumatic lines operate at 6 to 8 Bar (90-115 PSI). Uncontrolled release can launch steel fittings at high velocity.
- The 200-Ton press slide weighs 4.5 metric tons and will drop under gravity if hydraulic holding valves lose pressure.

SECTION 2: REQUIRED PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
1. Heavy-duty puncture-resistant nitrile gloves with reinforced palms (EN 388 Level 4).
2. Full wrap-around ANSI Z87.1 impact face shield over safety glasses when opening hydraulic bleeds.
3. ASTM F2413 metatarsal-guard safety work boots.
4. Chemical-resistant apron if draining hydraulic fluid reservoirs.

SECTION 3: STEP-BY-STEP FLUID POWER ZERO-ENERGY PROTOCOL

Step 1: Lower Machine Ram & Secure Mechanical Die Blocks
- Using manual jog mode, lower the press slide ram onto hardwood or certified steel safety drop blocks.
- Insert the electrical interlock safety plug connected to the die block safety chain into the press console receptacle. This electrically disables ram actuation.

Step 2: Isolate Primary Hydraulic Pump & Electric Motors
- Turn main hydraulic pump motor switch to "OFF" position.
- Lock out the electric supply disconnect at MCC-A (Breaker #8) using red padlock and danger tag.

Step 3: Depressurize High-Pressure Hydraulic Accumulators
- ⚠️ CRITICAL: Nitrogen-charged bladder accumulators ACC-1 and ACC-2 retain up to 180 Bar of stored energy even with the main pump turned off!
- Locate the manual accumulator safety shut-off block (Block HYD-SB-1).
- Slowly rotate the red manual bleed needle valve counter-clockwise by 2 full turns.
- Monitor hydraulic accumulator pressure gauge PG-ACC-1. Observe needle dropping to 0 Bar (0 PSI).
- Leave bleed valve in open position during servicing.

Step 4: Isolate & Lock Out Pneumatic Air Supply
- Locate the plant primary pneumatic supply drop at the FRL (Filter-Regulator-Lubricator) manifold.
- Rotate the yellow manual exhaust ball valve 90 degrees to the "VENT" position.
- Insert red lockout padlock through the valve lockout hole to prevent re-opening.
- The downstream air lines will automatically exhaust with an audible rush of air.

Step 5: Verify Complete Zero Energy State (Hydraulic & Pneumatic)
- Gauge Inspection: Inspect pressure gauge PG-MAIN (main pump), PG-ACC-1 (accumulator), and PG-PNEU (pneumatics).
- All three gauges MUST read exactly 0 Bar / 0 PSI.
- Actuate manual manual spool override buttons on directional control valves D1 and D2 to verify zero pilot pressure.
- Only when all gauges read zero and mechanical safety blocks are in place may hydraulic hose replacement, cylinder packing, or valve overhaul begin.
