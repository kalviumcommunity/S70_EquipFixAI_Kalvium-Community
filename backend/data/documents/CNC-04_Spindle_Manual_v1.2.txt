SECTION 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW AND OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATIONS
The CNC-04 High-Precision Vertical Machining Center is equipped with a direct-drive 15,000 RPM cartridge spindle. 
The spindle assembly consists of hybrid ceramic angular contact ball bearings, dual labyrinth air-purged seals, 
and an integrated thermistor loop for continuous temperature monitoring.

Operating Parameters:
- Maximum Rated Spindle Speed: 15,000 RPM
- Continuous Power Output: 22 kW
- Nominal Bearing Operating Temperature: 28°C - 42°C
- Maximum Allowable Spindle Runout (at spindle nose): 0.003 mm
- Required Coolant Flow: Minimum 15 L/min at 3.5 bar

SECTION 4: DIAGNOSTIC ALARMS AND ERROR CODES

### Error Code E-204: Spindle Bearing Over-Temperature & Excessive Vibration
Description:
The spindle diagnostic module triggers Alarm E-204 when the integrated PT100 thermistor registers a temperature 
exceeding 68°C for more than 4 seconds, or when accelerometer vibrations exceed 4.5 mm/s RMS.

Root Causes:
1. Spindle bearing race degradation, brinelling, or cage fracture due to operational fatigue or heavy shock loads.
2. Contamination of spindle bearing grease reservoir by water-soluble cutting coolant due to failed labyrinth air-seal pressure.
3. Lack of lubrication or blockage in the automatic grease dispenser unit (ISO VG 68 synthetic spindle grease).
4. Tool holder unbalance exceeding ISO 1940 Grade G2.5 at 12,000 RPM.

SECTION 6: STEP-BY-STEP REPAIR & BEARING REPLACEMENT PROCEDURE

For Error Code E-204 or bearing mechanical seizure, perform the following repair sequence:
Step 1: Perform complete electrical and pneumatic Lockout/Tagout according to Plant Safety SOP SP-12 before disassembling housing.
Step 2: Unclamp tool holder, disconnect spindle cooling lines, and drain residual coolant from the jacket.
Step 3: Remove the 8 front flange hex bolts using a 6mm Allen socket.
Step 4: Mount the hydraulic puller tool (Tool Code: PULL-CNC04) and extract the front bearing retainer collar.
Step 5: Inspect spindle shaft for runout or scoring. Measure runout using a 0.001mm dial test indicator. Maximum allowable shaft runout is 0.005 mm. If runout exceeds 0.008 mm, the spindle shaft must be re-ground.
Step 6: Install replacement matched bearing set (Spare Part: BRG-204-SKF, Matched Duplex Angular Contact Bearing Set).
Step 7: Re-grease bearings with precisely 4.2 grams of Klüberplex BEM 34-132 high-speed grease.
Step 8: Reinstall front flange bolts. Torque bolts in a criss-cross star pattern in two stages: initial torque to 20 Nm, final torque to 45 Nm.
Step 9: Reconnect thermistor harness TH-1. Verify sensor resistance is nominal 10.0 kΩ at 25°C ambient.

SECTION 9: PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE & TOLERANCES
- Every 200 Operating Hours: Inspect air-seal pressure regulator (minimum 0.25 MPa clean dry air required).
- Every 1,000 Operating Hours: Spindle vibration spectrum analysis (vibration velocity RMS must remain < 1.8 mm/s).
- Every 4,000 Operating Hours: Complete bearing runout inspection and lubrication reservoir flush.
- Lubrication Specification: Mobil Velocite No. 6 or Klüberplex BEM 34-132.
