# ROBOTIC WELDER CELL DAILY INSPECTION & CALIBRATION (SOP-ROB-01)
Equipment: 6-Axis Articulated Robotic Welding Cell (ROBOT-01)
Document ID: SOP-WELD-2026-V1
Classification: Standard Operating Procedure

SECTION 1: DAILY CELL INSPECTION & TORCH MAINTENANCE
1. Inspect torch tip nozzle for spatter accumulation. Clean with anti-spatter reamer and apply ceramic nozzle shield.
2. Verify contact tip diameter (Part: TIP-WELD-045). If bore elongation exceeds 1.4 mm, replace contact tip immediately.
3. Check wire feed drive rolls tension (gauge setting: 3.5 bar). Inspect guide liner for copper dusting or kinked wire.
4. Verify shielding gas flow rate: 18 to 22 CFH (75/25 Argon/CO2 mixture).

SECTION 2: CALIBRATION & TCP (TOOL CENTER POINT) ALIGNMENT
1. Mount pointer fixture to teach tool center point.
2. Run TCP automated calibration routine at 10% velocity override.
3. Verify positional repeat accuracy is within +/- 0.08 mm. If deviation exceeds 0.15 mm, perform 6-axis motor zeroing calibration.
